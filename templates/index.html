ABOUT CAMPUSBITE

Smart Canteen.
Simple Ordering.

CampusBite is a smart canteen order management system designed for students and campus canteens.

Students can browse the menu, add food to their cart, place orders and track their order status.

Our goal is to reduce waiting time and make canteen ordering simple, fast and convenient.
